### Problem Statement

E-commerce businesses often face difficulties in determining the appropriate inventory quantity due to uncertainty in future customer demand. Purchasing insufficient inventory may result in unmet customer demand and shortage-related costs, whereas purchasing excessive inventory can lead to unsold products and additional holding costs.

Traditional inventory decisions based on fixed demand estimates may not adequately account for variations in actual customer demand.

Therefore, this project aims to develop a stochastic optimization model for an e-commerce inventory management problem, where customer demand is treated as a random variable. Using probability distributions and Monte Carlo simulation, the model evaluates different inventory decisions and identifies the quantity that maximizes expected profit while considering purchasing costs, holding costs, and shortage penalties.

The performance of the stochastic optimization approach will also be compared with a deterministic inventory model to understand the impact of demand uncertainty on inventory decisions.
